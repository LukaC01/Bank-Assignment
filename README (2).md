# 💳 Financial Data Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

This project was developed as part of a **Junior Financial Data Analyst** technical assessment for a bank / FinTech environment.

The main goal of the analysis is to understand **what drives revenue, where the main risks are, how products and customer segments perform, and what actions could improve the business**.

The project focuses on transforming financial data into an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to provide management with clear, actionable insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer three main business questions:

1. **What drives revenue?**
2. **Where are the main risks?**
3. **How can business performance be improved?**

The dashboard evaluates the business across different **customer segments, products, and performance indicators** to identify the most valuable areas and potential risks.

---

## 📊 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on answering:

- Which **segments and products** are the most valuable?
- Where is the **risk highest**?
- How is the **performance of different products**?
- What are the **main problems** affecting the business?
- What **solutions and improvements** could be suggested to management?

---

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

An interactive dashboard was developed in **Power BI** to present the most important information in a clear and management-friendly format.

### Features:

- KPI cards for key financial metrics
- Product performance analysis
- Customer segment comparison
- Revenue analysis
- Risk analysis
- Comparative charts and visualizations
- Interactive filters and slicers
- Visual breakdowns of important business indicators
- Management-oriented views for identifying problems and opportunities

The dashboard is designed to allow users to quickly move from **high-level business performance** to more detailed analysis of individual products and segments.

---

## 🔎 Analysis Approach

The analysis follows a structured process:

### 1. Data Preparation
- Reviewed the available financial data
- Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis
- Checked data quality and consistency
- Prepared relevant fields and metrics for visualization

### 2. Exploratory Analysis
- Compared products and customer segments
- Examined revenue and performance patterns
- Identified areas with higher levels of risk
- Looked for significant differences between business segments

### 3. Dashboard Development
- Selected the most relevant KPIs
- Created diverse and content-rich visuals
- Added interactive filtering
- Organized the dashboard around management-level questions

### 4. Business Recommendations
The final stage focuses on translating the analytical findings into practical recommendations, including opportunities to:

- Improve product performance
- Focus on the most valuable customer segments
- Reduce exposure to high-risk areas
- Improve underperforming products
- Allocate resources more effectively
